# Rules and Gameplay

Valentina Noir is based on a customized version of the World of Darkness system. The changes from the canonical World of Darkness system are the result of a long running campaign that has been in progress in some form or another for over 30 years.

## Philosophy

Valentina Noir is designed to be an aid in running World of Darkness campaigns for players who interact in-person when gaming. The goal is to create a system that is easy to use for the Storyteller and players while they are in a live game and to track progress between sessions. As such, certain mechanics are omitted on purpose which are best managed by the Storyteller or players themselves when in a live game and which are subject to frequent changes. Some examples of these excluded mechanics are:

-   Blood pool
-   Hunger
-   Rouse checks
-   Health level management
-   Gnosis
-   Combat

## World of Darkness Editions

We support the following World of Darkness editions and do our best to support character's created for each of these editions.

-   World of Darkness v4
-   World of Darkness v5

## Character Classes

Current supported character classes are:

-   Vampire
-   Werewolf
-   Hunter
-   Mortal
-   Mage (Partial support)
-   Ghoul

## Experience

Experience in Valentina Noir is granted to the player, not to specific characters. Experience is tracked at the campaign level.

There are two types of experience that can be granted to a player:

-   **Experience Points** - The basic currency of character advancement.
-   **Cool Points** - Cool points are worth `10` experience points and can be awarded by the Storyteller as a reward to players who have roleplayed exceptionally well.
